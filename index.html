AI/ML Research Engineer · Cupertino, CA

AI/ML research engineer specializing in generative AI, multimodal large language models, 3D computer vision, and robotics, with a decade of experience taking ideas from research to production. I lead projects, mentor engineers, and enjoy hard problems that need both rigorous science and solid engineering, especially ones that make a meaningful impact on people's lives.

My work ships in products used by millions, including GenAI capabilities on Amazon Echo Show, the intelligence and identity security ML stack behind IDs in Apple Wallet, Apple HomeView, and Face Unlock on iPhone Duo.

Selected research

Ongoing theme

Human-like learning systems

  • Machines need hundreds of examples to learn a class; humans need a few, or just a description
  • Few-, one-, and zero-shot learning as a path to continual, human-like learning
WACV 2021

One-shot prototypical encoders

  • Predicts class prototypes from raw images via image-to-image translation
  • Reduces latent-space hubness
  • Adds contrastive and multi-task losses to sharpen few-shot discrimination
Read paper →
FG 2020

Feature selection for zero-shot gesture recognition

  • Compares heuristic, raw-velocity, and deep-network features
  • Linear and bilinear models that jointly optimize semantic and classification losses
Read paper →
IEEE THMS 2020

Agreement analysis with gesture description vectors

  • Builds gesture descriptors directly into agreement analysis
  • Introduces the soft agreement rate (SAR)
  • Shows existing agreement metrics are a special case
Read paper →
FG 2019

Zero-shot gesture dataset

  • First annotated attribute database for ChaLearn 2013 and MSRC-12 gestures
  • Built from linguistics literature and Amazon Mechanical Turk annotation

DESK: surgical skill transfer across robots
